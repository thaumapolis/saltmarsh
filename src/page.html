What you are looking at

The ground is a marsh cross-section: a deep channel, a broad flat, and banks. The coloured surface is water. Vertical height is exaggerated about 22× — real relief here is 0.5% of the width and would look flat.

What you can do

  • Toggle INTAKE and OUTFALL. Nothing happens unless there is a head difference, so timing matters more than the buttons.
  • Drag the view to rotate it, pinch or scroll to zoom.
  • Watch the fish counter. Fish come onto the flats to feed as the tide floods. If you drain the pool faster than they can swim back to the channel, they are stranded in puddles and die.

Why it behaves like this

The numbers behind the tides

One file, no network, no storage. The simulation is a 96×96 grid advanced by shallow-water exchange; the view is drawn on the CPU because there is no GPU path here.
